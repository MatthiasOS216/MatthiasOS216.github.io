02 / 07 · Calendar and booking

Metona

In development · ELVN OS

A clearer relationship with your time.

Metona is the calendar being shaped for ELVN OS: a place to see the day, understand your commitments and plan around the life you actually live.

Your time, in context.

The direction is simple: bring the calendar closer to the work it belongs to.

See the day ahead.

The current calendar view separates all-day items from timed commitments inside Cortex.

Keep plans close to tasks.

Moving between your task list and calendar is part of the current interface, so the two views are easy to find.

Make room for what comes next.

A fuller calendar and booking experience is the product direction. Booking and additional calendar views are not presented as available features.

Where it stands.

Metona is in development. Its approved identity and calendar interface are in place; this is not a finished standalone calendar or a live booking service.

A dedicated interface preview is forthcoming.

A product showcase. Public access is not open.
