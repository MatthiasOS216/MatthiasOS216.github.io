Synapse

Planned · ELVN OS

Remember the person. Keep the connection.

Synapse is the relationship workspace envisioned for ELVN OS: a place for the context, promises and next steps that make a follow-up feel personal.

Relationships with their context intact.

The aim is to help you return to a conversation without starting from zero.

Remember what matters.

The product direction brings people and the context around them into one relationship view.

Keep promises in sight.

Follow-ups are intended to connect what was discussed with what needs to happen next.

Find the next conversation.

A relationship workspace should make it easier to see who to reconnect with and why, without reducing people to a spreadsheet.

Where it stands.

Synapse remains listed as Planned in the ELVN app lineup. Interface and integration work exists, but this page describes the product direction rather than a publicly available standalone app.

A public interface preview will follow when the presentation experience is ready.

A product showcase. Public access is not open.
