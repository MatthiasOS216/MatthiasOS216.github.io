House of Data

In development · ELVN OS

A home for what you create.

House of Data is the storage product taking shape within ELVN OS. Its purpose is to bring your saved work into a place you can understand, organize and return to.

Built around the things you keep.

The direction starts with ownership and continuity: your work should remain useful long after the moment you save it.

Keep the work together.

The vision is a home for files and saved material across the way you work.

Find it in your own words.

Asking for what you need, instead of remembering where it went, is part of the intended experience. Natural-language retrieval is not yet offered here.

Choose where it lives.

Local, home-server, hosted and hybrid storage are product directions. These are not claims of working sync, backup or cloud hosting.

Where it stands.

House of Data is in development. The name and visual identity are approved; a finished storage application has not been verified for this showcase.

The approved artwork introduces the product. An application screenshot will be added when a working interface is ready.

A product showcase. Public access is not open.
