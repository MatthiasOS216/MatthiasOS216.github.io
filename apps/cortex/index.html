Cortex

In development · ELVN OS

Make room for the next thing.

A place to turn everything on your mind into a day you can actually work through. Cortex brings tasks, habits and routines together, with space for what matters now and what can wait.

From a full mind to a clear next step.

Keep the detail when you need it. Keep the next action visible when you don’t.

Capture it before it disappears.

Collect tasks in an inbox, then add notes, dates, lists, tags and smaller steps. The details stay with the work.

Give your day a shape.

Sort work into Now, Next and Later. Start, snooze or reschedule a task as the day changes.

Build a rhythm you can return to.

Track habits and move through routines one timed step at a time, with room to pause and pick things up again.

Where it stands.

The task and habit foundation is built as a local prototype. Cortex is still in development; the full experience and its connections across ELVN are not a public release.

Interface photography will be added as the presentation build is prepared.

A product showcase. Public access is not open.
