Metona privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Metona is ELVN's calendar and booking app. It runs on the computer of the person who operates it, and it keeps what it holds there. This page says what it reads, what it stores, and what it never does.

What Metona is

Metona is a personal calendar and booking tool made by ELVN. You sign in, link the calendars you already use, and share booking pages so people can book time with you. It is not an advertising product and it has no business model built on your information.

What it collects

When you sign in with Google
Your name and your email address, to recognise you. Signing in asks for nothing else. It does not open any calendar.
When you link a calendar
You are sent to Google and asked separately. If you agree, Metona can read the events on the calendars you choose, and add or change the events it creates there. It reads titles, times, places and whether you are busy, so that it can show your day and keep booking pages from offering times you are taken. Calendars you leave switched off are not used.
When someone books you
The name, email address, time zone and any answers or note the visitor types into the booking page. These go to the person whose page it is, so they can prepare and manage the booking.
Account settings
Your working hours, booking types and preferences, which you set yourself.

How the Google permissions are used

  • openid, email and profile: to sign you in.
  • calendar.readonly: to list your calendars and read their events, so other accounts' meetings can close those times on your booking pages.
  • calendar.events: to add the events Metona makes (such as bookings) and plain "Busy" blocks to calendars you pick. A "Busy" block carries no title, place or notes from the other calendar.

Metona does not read your email, your contacts, your files or anything else in your Google account.

Metona's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information from Google is used only to provide and improve the calendar and booking features you see in Metona.

Where it is stored

Metona stores its data in a database file on the computer that runs it. Sign-in tokens and calendar passwords are encrypted with a key kept on that same computer. Metona does not copy your calendar data to a cloud service run by ELVN.

Other ELVN apps on the same computer can ask Metona for your calendar, but only with a token that you create and can cancel.

What it never does

  • It does not sell your information or rent it to anyone.
  • It does not share Google data with other companies, advertisers or data brokers.
  • It does not use your calendar to show ads or to build advertising profiles.
  • It does not allow people at ELVN to read your calendar. They would see it only if you showed it to them, for example to fix a problem you reported.
  • It does not send marketing email. If the person who runs it has turned email on, it emails you only about your own bookings: a confirmation, any change or cancellation, and reminders.

Who can see what

A visitor to your booking page sees the open times you offer, and nothing about why other times are closed. After booking, they see their own booking and can change or cancel it through a private link. The events on your calendars are visible to you, and to no one else through Metona.

Your choices

  • Unlink a calendar in Metona's Linked calendars screen. Metona forgets that account's saved sign-in and removes the events and "Busy" blocks it had added there.
  • Take back Google's permission at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Metona's access ends, and any sign-in it already holds stops working within the hour.
  • Ask for your data to be deleted. Tell whoever operates your copy of Metona, and they can remove your account and its bookings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and anything that would use your information in a new way will ask you first.
